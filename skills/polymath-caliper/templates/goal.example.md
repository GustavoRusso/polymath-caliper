# The Goal (System Spine)

Copy this file to `personal/goal.md` during `init`. There is only **one Goal** per instance. It is the spine that gives purpose to every Focus Area.

`personal/goal.md` is gitignored and must not be committed.

## 3. Focus Areas Serving This Goal

Each Focus Area has its own file under `personal/focus-areas/<slug>.md` and is assigned to one lane:

| Focus Area | File | Lane (`Build` / `Reading` / `Open` / `Parking Lot`) | Role in reaching The Goal |
|---|---|---|---|
| `<FOCUS_AREA_1>` | `personal/focus-areas/<SLUG_1>.md` | `<LANE>` | `<HOW_IT_HELPS_REACH_THE_GOAL>` |
| `<FOCUS_AREA_2>` | `personal/focus-areas/<SLUG_2>.md` | `<LANE>` | `<HOW_IT_HELPS_REACH_THE_GOAL>` |

### Lane notes

- **Build:** Work that creates verifiable proof now (projects, deliverables, outreach/positioning materials).
- **Reading:** Slow, continuous study that supports the Goal.
- **Open:** At most one selected exploration topic at a time.
- **Parking Lot:** Valuable ideas without current commitment. Competing choices stay here until you pick one for Open.
